# v1.15 scope — noise-twin calibration (assurance cycle)

**Status:** Q15.2 / Q15.5 / Q15.6 / Q15.7 direction approved 2026-09-27;
numerical protocol pending must-pin review. Revised against `f6b9d4b` to
retain the historical corpus and separate it from prospective validation.
Originally written 2026-09-27 against `1661d23`. Mirrors `docs/v1.14-scope.md` (why-now →
structural facts → forks Q15.x with a recommendation each → assurance →
risks → out of scope → sequence).

**How this cycle differs from v1.14.** The measurement this cycle was chosen
to make has, in effect, already run: seven weeks of nightly telemetry sit in
the Actions logs, never read. §2 records what they show. Those numbers were
derived with the data in view, so they are recorded as structural facts, not
as a pre-committed judgment. The one decision below that depends on a
threshold — the candidate table in Q15.5 — does not bind until a frozen
numerical protocol, prospective confirmation (Q15.6), sensitivity controls
(Q15.7) and a separate activation review have satisfied their criteria.

**No crate change, no version bump, no publish** (the v0.14 / v1.10 / v1.14
assurance-cycle precedent). The work is:

1. **Record the calibration result.** `noise_twin_class_split` does not track
   the excess it was built to calibrate. Criterion (1) of the 2026-06-17
   re-promotion bar fails by an order of magnitude, and the relative-gate
   design closes as a documented negative. This does not rule out every
   materially different future reference.
2. **Record why no successor twin is proposed.** The excess is specific to
   the target *and* the CPU model; the existing reference does not track it.
3. **Disposition the 2026-09-17 nightly red.** It sat unrecorded for ten
   days: a `ct_fp_invert` sentinel breach on no-change code, on EPYC 9V74
   only. That establishes an alarm on unchanged code, not a harmless cause.
4. **Replace the relative-gate idea with a per-(target, SKU) bound table**
   candidate for the four demoted targets, retaining the fanout's existing
   policy. A frozen rule and fresh evidence precede any activation; the
   study may conclude with no gate change.

---

## 1. Why now

The twin landed with #149 (2026-08-11; design
`docs/2026-08-10-assurance-hardening-design.md`) as **required telemetry**:
its presence gates, and its value is print-only "until several GitHub-hosted
nightly runs establish whether it tracks the observed class-split noise".
The bar it has to clear was written on 2026-06-17
(`docs/v0.5-dudect-recalibration.md`, "The noise-twin"):

1. multi-night / multi-image co-spike correlation;
2. injected-leak tests showing a real invert / sign / HMAC leak still fails
   under the relative rule;
3. a written soundness argument that the twin cannot absorb a real leak.

Real coverage waits on it. `SECURITY.md` carries two honest residuals
pinned to "until the class-split-aware noise twin is calibrated":

- a medium `k`-only nonce leak in [0.25, 0.55] has no authoritative gate
  (`ct_sign_k_class` is on the 0.55 sentinel);
- the HMAC-SM3 primitive has no direct non-composite gate (`ct_hmac_sm3` is
  on the sentinel). The TLCP key schedule's HMAC claim rides on it.

`.claude/rules/dudect.md` also ties any F21 telemetry target to the same
runner calibration.

Meanwhile the data accumulated and nobody read it: 63 twin-era nightly runs.
The 2026-09-17 red went unrecorded, and `docs/v1.14-scope.md` §1, written on
2026-09-23, says "nightly fuzz and dudect green", which was not true of
dudect. Reading the corpus is the calibration.

## 2. Structural facts (verified 2026-09-27)

### 2.1 The corpus

- **Source.** The gate output of every `dudect-nightly.yml` run that
  reached its gate, from the first run carrying the twin (2026-08-11, run `31461679436` at
  `1a05287`) through 2026-09-27 (run `36296678111` at `746b3fb`):
  **63 runs, 252 jobs**, four feature legs, 100K×5 each. Eight further
  dispatched runs (32 jobs) against the `v1.11.0` tag predate the twin and
  are excluded from every twin statistic.
- **CPU labels.** **176 jobs** (44 runs, 2026-08-22 onward) carry
  `RUNNER-CPU`; the 76 earlier jobs are unlabelled. Labelled share: EPYC
  7763 90, EPYC 9V74 45, Xeon 8573C 16, EPYC 9V45 9, Xeon 6973P-C 9,
  Xeon 8370C 7.
- **No-change.** The corpus ran on 21 commits. Against `746b3fb`, each has
  **zero non-comment line changes** in `sm2/`, `sm3/`, `hmac.rs` and
  `kdf.rs`: 18 were checked by diff, and the other 3 are deleted-branch
  commits whose changed-file lists touch no `crates/` path. The
  `timing_leaks.rs` edits in the window are doc comments. The pins
  `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@1.95.0` and `runs-on: ubuntu-24.04` are
  unchanged. Every reading of the four demoted targets and the twin
  therefore measures identical code.
- **Harness alive.** `negative_control`'s per-run minimum over all 252 jobs
  is 20.05 (gate `> 1.0`).
- **Extraction.** The gate lines were pulled from each job log through the
  Actions API and parsed from the printed `runs=[…]` lists, which the gate
  already prints as absolute values. Every target was asserted to carry
  five values per job. One job was cross-checked digit-for-digit against an
  independent fetch.

### 2.2 The twin does not track (criterion 1)

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| twin job-median, all 252 jobs | median 0.0057, p90 0.0081, **max 0.0342** |
| twin single runs (1 260) | median 0.0057, p99 0.0643, max 0.4039 (one isolated run, 2026-09-18, EPYC 7763; that job's median 0.0062) |
| **episodes**: a demoted target's job-median > 0.20 | **161**, in 131 jobs |
| twin job-median in those 131 jobs | **max 0.0198** |
| Spearman ρ, twin vs target job-medians (n = 252) | `ct_fn_invert` −0.07, `ct_fp_invert` −0.18, `ct_sign_k_class` −0.13, `ct_hmac_sm3` +0.11 |

The twin reads the same on every SKU (job-median 0.005–0.008; max ≤ 0.034)
and never follows the excess. It sits an order of magnitude below the
episode floor. Adjusting a multiplier or absolute floor could change a
threshold, but would not establish that the reference tracks these events.
That is the relevant v0.19 fix-vs-fix failure again, now with two distinct inputs.

Job-median of each demoted target by CPU class (labelled jobs;
median / p90 / **max**, with n):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ct_fn_invert` | .008/.011/**.027** | .185/.339/**.459** | .008/.011/**.013** | .010/.021/**.021** | .010/.019/**.019** | .011/.015/**.015** |
| `ct_fp_invert` | .024/.073/**.221** | .215/.376/**.618** | .036/.050/**.050** | .019/.028/**.028** | .015/.022/**.022** | .040/.044/**.044** |
| `ct_sign_k_class` | .210/.269/**.311** | .176/.197/**.214** | .020/.034/**.035** | .225/.383/**.383** | .022/.031/**.031** | .171/.179/**.179** |
| `ct_hmac_sm3` | .028/.297/**.432** | .011/.018/**.021** | .042/.071/**.098** | .010/.013/**.013** | .018/.134/**.134** | .016/.037/**.037** |

- The **invert pair** is loud on EPYC 9V74 (19 + 25 episodes) and quiet
  elsewhere. The only other excursion is one `ct_fp_invert` job-median of
  0.221 on 7763.
- **`ct_sign_k_class`** sits near 0.2 as a *level*, not a spike, on every
  AMD class and on Xeon 8370C. It is quiet (≤ 0.035) on Xeon 8573C and
  6973P-C.
- **`ct_hmac_sm3`** spikes on EPYC 7763 (13 episodes, max 0.432) and is
  quiet on 9V74, 9V45 and 8370C.
- The four do not share a factor. ρ(fn, fp) = +0.57, but ρ(fn, hmac) =
  −0.44, ρ(fp, hmac) = −0.37 and ρ(sign_k, hmac) = +0.01. These pooled
  correlations motivate per-target and per-CPU analysis; they do not prove
  that every possible reference must fail.

Since 2026-05 the ledger has called this "image noise" and "two-input
class-split noise". The table shows a stable property of **code path ×
class inputs × CPU model** in this corpus. It does not establish a universal
impossibility result for shared references.

**The mechanism is not established here.** One published class of cause
fits the pattern: operand-value-dependent power and frequency behaviour (the
Hertzbleed class), which can give instruction-level constant-time code
value-dependent timing on some parts. The twin could not exercise it. Its
inputs `0x36…` / `0xc9…` are bitwise complements with equal Hamming weight,
and its body is 256 rotate-xor steps. By contrast, `ct_sign_k_class` splits
`k ∈ {1, 3}` against `k ≈ n`, and `ct_fp_invert` splits `0x1234` against a
full-width value. This is a hypothesis for a possible research follow-up,
not a finding.

### 2.4 The 2026-09-17 red (run `35185121870`)

At `6fa066d` (docs only; `crates/` identical to published 1.13.0 at
`5c0ae59`), the fourth leg drew an **EPYC 9V74**:

```text
SENTINEL FAIL: ct_fp_invert median|tau|=0.6183 > 0.55 — gross regression, investigate
runs = 0.7117, 0.2494, 0.1479, 0.6183, 0.7010
```

- Same job: `ct_fn_invert` 0.2879, twin 0.0077.
- Same night, the other three legs (Xeon 8370C, EPYC 7763, Xeon 8573C) read
  `ct_fp_invert` 0.0187 / 0.0199 / 0.0501.

**Disposition: an unexplained SKU-specific sentinel breach on unchanged
code.** It challenges the old no-change envelope; it does not prove a false
positive or exclude a microarchitecture-specific leak. The 0.55 bound was
set above a no-change envelope of "~0.40" and below the v0.1 reference leak
"~0.70". Here no-change single runs reached 0.7117; these are not the gated
job-median, which was 0.6183. `ct_fn_invert` on the same SKU has reached a
0.459 median (2026-08-30). Keep the observation and investigate its cause;
do not absorb it into a relaxed bound or remove it from the corpus merely
to get a passing table. Any gate change waits for Q15.5–Q15.7.

### 2.5 Today's gates and constraints

- **The four demoted targets** are PR-smoke telemetry, plus the nightly
  median-gated sentinel at 0.55.
- **The twin** is required telemetry and print-only. It is locked there by
  `.github/scripts/check_assurance_policy.py`, whose mutation suite rejects
  promoting it into any gate map.
- **`ct_sm4_cbc_decrypt_fanout`** gates at 0.20, or 0.55 on EPYC 9V74
  (#184, 2026-09-01). Its revisit criterion reads: *"If a second SKU shows
  the same behaviour, do not stack a second string-match — that is the
  point to design the per-class floor properly (measured floors per class,
  versioned in one table …)."* §2.3–§2.4 are the same situation, for four
  more targets.
- **Editing the gates.** Any gate edit regenerates the four reviewed
  fingerprints with the script's own helpers and is validated on the PR
  smoke run and a dispatched nightly (`.claude/rules/dudect.md`).
- **Budget.** The `default` leg's harness step took 37 min 25 s of the
  40-minute timeout on 2026-09-27, so a new target would need a timeout
  change, which is itself pinned.

## 3. Forks

### Q15.1 — What v1.15 is

An **assurance cycle**: record the result, investigate the red, and evaluate
a candidate per-SKU table under a predeclared protocol. Workspace stays
`1.13.0`, with no CHANGELOG entry and no publish. Study evidence can land
without activation. Only an eligible result permits a separate activation
PR, whose potential files are:

- the nightly Parse-and-gate Python and its fingerprints;
- the recalibration ledger;
- `SECURITY.md`'s coverage text for the four targets and the twin;
- `.claude/rules/dudect.md`;
- `CLAUDE.md`;
- one evidence CSV (Q15.10).

**Recommendation: as stated; nothing under `crates/`.**

### Q15.2 — Verdict on the twin as a relative-gate reference

**Approved:** criterion (1) fails for the current twin-based design; retire
that design. The falsified v0.19 fix-vs-fix rule stays retired too. Merely
collecting more nights or changing a multiplier on the same reference is
not a new design.

This is not "closed for good" for every possible relative approach.
Reopening requires a materially different reference, fresh hosted-runner
calibration and injected-leak controls demonstrating that the reference
cannot absorb a real regression. There is no need to run controls for the
already rejected relative rule; Q15.7 independently applies to validating
any replacement gate. Keep the paired corpus evidence, including strata
and episode definition, behind this negative result.

### Q15.3 — A successor twin?

- **(a)** None in v1.15.
- **(b)** A factorial research twin, meaning Hamming-weight-imbalanced
  inputs × a target-length body, as extra telemetry.

Against (b):

- **Cost.** It adds work to a `default` leg with only ~2.5 minutes of
  observed timeout headroom; its actual runtime has not been measured.
- **Reach.** Tracking one target on one SKU would not establish coverage of
  the other target/SKU combinations (§2.3); each would need validation.
- **Soundness.** Per-target twins shaped to each target's operand profile
  make criterion (3), that the twin cannot absorb a real leak, hardest
  exactly where it matters.

**Recommendation: (a).** A mechanism study, if ever wanted, belongs off the
gate path.

### Q15.4 — The twin itself

- **(a)** Keep it as required telemetry, reframed as calibrated negative
  evidence — the v0.19 probes' precedent, which are kept as "the evidence
  that the noise is class-split-specific".
- **(b)** Delete it. That is a crate change (the bench) plus surgery on the
  policy verifier's mutation suite.

**Recommendation: (a).** Its bench doc comment ("non-blocking pending
calibration") goes stale. Leave it: correcting it would put a `crates/`
diff into a no-crate cycle and re-owe Gate #1 for no behaviour change.
Record it for the next crate-touching cycle.

### Q15.5 — A frozen per-(target, CPU model) candidate table

**Approved:** a versioned, deterministic historical derivation followed by
prospective confirmation. This is a static candidate table, not a rule that
learns a more permissive threshold from each new red.

Before executing the derivation, pin the corpus cutoff, input identities,
eligible code/harness versions and targets, exclusion rules, minimum
independent observations, statistic, exact formula and constants, rounding,
ceiling and fallback. Preserve excluded observations and their reasons.
Unexplained reds remain investigation items: neither silently discard them
nor absorb them into a higher baseline. Unchanged code does not establish
absence of leakage.

The displayed key may be `(target, CPU model)`, but applicability also
includes feature leg, declared sample budget, aggregation, toolchain and
runner image. Do not pool incompatible strata. Any pooling needs a
predeclared justification; the §2 pooled counts alone do not establish
eligibility. A matching CPU string does not validate a different environment.
The existing nightly statistic remains the median of five absolute reported
tau values; those five bench passes count as one job observation. Pin how
reruns, manual dispatches, duplicates, missing outputs and timeouts are
handled, and justify independence across selected observations.

Sparse, unknown or incompatible cells keep their current policy. The four
demoted targets are not re-promoted merely because historical cells look
quiet; a proposed promotion must be explicitly included in the reviewed
numerical protocol and validated for its stated claim. Fanout retains its
existing 0.20 / 9V74 0.55 policy during the study. PR-smoke is separate.

**Disposition of the first draft's rule.** Its ≥ 8 pooled jobs and
maximum-based cuts (≤ 0.10 → 0.20, ≤ 0.55 → 0.55, otherwise telemetry)
were exploratory choices made with the data visible. They are not the
approved derivation rule. In particular, a sentinel breach does not
mechanically authorize removing that sentinel. Retain the first draft's
output below as an exploratory record, not an activation candidate:

| target | exploratory 0.20 cells | exploratory 0.55 cells | proposed removal, not authorized | sparse fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ct_fn_invert` | 7763, 8573C, 9V45, 6973P-C | 9V74 | — | 8370C |
| `ct_fp_invert` | 8573C, 9V45, 6973P-C | 7763 | 9V74 | 8370C |
| `ct_sign_k_class` | 8573C, 6973P-C | 7763, 9V74, 9V45 | — | 8370C |
| `ct_hmac_sm3` | 9V74, 8573C, 9V45 | 7763, 6973P-C | — | 8370C |

A gate passing on another SKU cannot establish detection on a noisy SKU.
No probability of drawing a quiet runner closes either `SECURITY.md`
residual; coverage statements must follow demonstrated per-cell sensitivity.

**Must pin before calibration code or measurement:**

| Item | Required concrete protocol content |
|---|---|
| Corpus | UTC cutoff, run/attempt identities, eligible code and environments, exclusions and missing-data accounting |
| Derivation | Target/configuration set, CPU matching, independent-observation minimum, exact formula/constants, rounding, ceiling, fallback |
| Confirmation | Candidate-table identity, future start, maximum duration, minimum per-cell observations, per-cell and table-wide acceptance criteria, proposed activation set |
| Sensitivity | Per-cell control matrix, minimum effects to detect, patch/build identities, scheduling, repetitions and detection criteria |
| Drift | Applicability checks, invalidation rules and disposition of every affected cell |

These numerical choices remain a protocol-design task; approval of the four
direction decisions does not silently approve the first draft's constants.

### Q15.6 — Prospective confirmation and predeclared outcomes

**Approved:** freeze the rule, candidate table and acceptance criteria before
starting a fresh confirmation window. Existing workflow gates remain
authoritative; candidate verdicts run in shadow mode. A current-gate failure
still requires investigation even if the candidate passes.

Pin both a minimum number of independent eligible observations per cell and
a maximum calendar window, with per-cell and table-wide acceptance criteria.
Predeclare missing/invalid-run handling, environment drift and whether
partial activation is permitted. Do not stop upon a convenient green run,
cherry-pick successful cells, or extend the window until a rare CPU passes.

The original “five new jobs or 28 nights from spec merge” proposal is
superseded. Merge of the unpinned draft does not start this window; any data
already inspected belongs to exploration. A failed cell does **not** fall
one tier and become confirmed on the same data.

Record exactly one table-level outcome, with a disposition for every row:

| Outcome | Predeclared condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible for activation review | Coverage, prospective acceptance and required sensitivity criteria all pass for the predeclared activation set | Submit the frozen table and evidence in a separate activation PR |
| Rejected | A rejection criterion is met, including required leak controls failing their detection criterion | Keep current policy and record the failure |
| Insufficient evidence | Required observations, comparable environments or sensitivity evidence are unavailable at the deadline, with no rejection criterion met | Keep current policy and name the missing evidence |

Rejection takes precedence over insufficient evidence. Changing the formula,
control matrix or acceptance criteria requires a new protocol version and
fresh prospective data; retain the old outcome. No automatic relaxation,
retuning or activation follows a window result.

### Q15.7 — Injected-leak controls before activation

**Approved:** retain target-specific injected-leak controls as a requirement
before activating relaxed cells. Ordinary-run observations characterize
alarm frequency; they do not establish detection sensitivity. Removing a
sentinel entirely is a relaxation and cannot pass a requirement to detect
leaks in that cell. If no useful gate can be validated, report the gap and
seek a separate explicit coverage decision rather than silently activating
telemetry-only status.

Use isolated research builds (the former option (c)), not new leaky targets
in the shipped crate tree. Run the same measurement window, input classes,
sample budget, aggregation and candidate verdict path as the proposed gate.
Predeclare controls covering every relaxed cell, or a justified equivalence
before measurement; another CPU's passing result is not evidence of this
CPU's sensitivity. Re-promoted cells also require evidence appropriate to
the restored detection claim, not just a quiet baseline.

Include modest relevant injected effects that a relaxed threshold could
conceal as well as gross positive controls. Pin intended minimum effects,
control identities, repetition counts and detection criteria before running
them. Do not increase the injected effect after failure and call that
validation of the original claim. Inability to separate baseline and the
required leak effects means the proposed gate is unsuitable for that cell.

The existing per-pass `negative_control` remains mandatory. It establishes
basic harness operation, not sensitivity in each affected target's window.
Leaky patches must never enter shipped crate sources or release artifacts.

A baseline-only study may finish with a candidate table and prospective
alarm-frequency results. Without the required controls, mark sensitivity
unvalidated and activation deferred; a green baseline does not waive this
requirement.

### Q15.8 — Where the table applies

**Recommendation: the nightly only.** PR-smoke runs 10K×3, where
sensitivity and envelope differ and this corpus holds no PR-smoke data, so
the four targets stay PR telemetry.

### Q15.11 — Revisit rules

A breach triggers investigation and a ledger entry, not automatic movement
from 0.20 to 0.55 or from sentinel to telemetry. Preserve current policy
until a reviewed change satisfies Q15.5–Q15.7. Never merge with an unexplained
red; a green rerun on different hardware does not resolve it.

New SKUs and sparse cells retain their existing fallback. Creating,
tightening or relaxing a cell requires the applicable protocol evidence;
no automatic “eight jobs” eligibility survives Q15.5. Environment drift
invalidates applicability until reviewed. Any revised table needs fresh
prospective validation and sensitivity evidence for its changed claims.

## 4. Assurance

- **No change under `crates/` or to `Cargo.toml`.** Gate #1, the
  public-API baseline and semver are untouched.
- **Any eligible activation edit** changes only the nightly workflow's
  Parse-and-gate policy; PR-smoke retains its own policy. Preserve fanout
  behavior if its block is folded into the table. Recompute affected reviewed
  fingerprints with the policy script's helpers, pass its mutation suite,
  and validate PR smoke plus a dispatched nightly before merge.
  Fabricated-log fixtures must follow the approved numerical protocol:
  below/at/above-bound verdicts, sparse/unknown/incompatible fallback,
  incomplete measurement failure, per-pass liveness failure and rejection
  of unvalidated overrides. Do not assume a 0.20 cell or a telemetry-only
  removal before those policies have been justified and reviewed.
- **Evidence** lives in the ledger entry, the corpus CSV (Q15.10), the
  window evaluation (Q15.6) and the control results (Q15.7). Fixture logs
  check verdict plumbing; they do not replace injected-leak measurements.
- **Disclosure boundary** is clean at P1 on every PR of the cycle. Run IDs
  and CPU model strings only; no host paths.

## 5. Risks

- **Pool drift.** Quiet SKUs can leave the pool (9V45 arrived on
  2026-09-01), and coverage then drops silently. The `SKU-GATE:` lines make
  it visible, and the ledger should re-read the SKU mix at each cycle
  start.
- **Knife-edge exploratory cells.** `ct_hmac_sm3` on 8573C has a max of
  0.098 against the first draft's 0.10 cut; this is a reason to scrutinize
  the derivation, not to treat that cut as already approved.
- **Model-string matching.** A new stepping with the same model name may
  differ materially. Applicability and drift rules must acknowledge what
  metadata the runner exposes and keep unvalidated environments on the
  existing policy.
- **Post-hoc construction.** The rule was written after reading the data.
  The prospective window, with committed outcomes, is the guard, and so is
  freezing the revised derivation before generating its candidate table.
  The first draft's cuts and pooled counts are not an accepted protocol.
- **The mechanism is unknown** (§2.3). A future `SECURITY.md` update must
  distinguish observed alarms and validated sensitivity from the cause of
  an effect. Passing hosted-runner gates does not certify absence of either
  code-level or platform-dependent timing leakage.

## 6. Out of scope

- A twin v2 or mechanism study (Q15.3).
- New shipped synthetic-leak targets. Isolated control builds are in scope
  under Q15.7.
- Any PR-smoke change (Q15.8).
- Timeout, toolchain or runner-pin moves.
- Self-hosted runners.
- AVX-512 `sbox_x64`.
- An F21 telemetry target. This study does not calibrate its narrow window
  or discharge its separate hosted-runner requirements.

## 7. Sequence

1. **Scope revision:** retain the historical corpus, record the four approved
   direction decisions and correct unsupported inference from SKU-specific
   alarms. No workflow or security-claim changes.
2. **Numerical protocol:** prepare the concrete pins in Q15.5 for must-pin
   review before calibration code, derivation or new study measurements.
   Preserve the historical evidence before source logs expire.
3. **Candidate and validation:** derive and freeze the candidate table;
   collect fresh prospective observations and execute the predeclared
   isolated control matrix. Record all exclusions and row dispositions.
4. **Report:** exactly one Q15.6 outcome. A study with insufficient evidence

5. **Activation, only if eligible:** a separate PR updates the nightly gate,
   fingerprints and public coverage text consistently, and validates policy
   checks, PR smoke and a dispatched nightly. No automatic activation.
6. **Close:** record the actual study outcome in `version-history.md`.
