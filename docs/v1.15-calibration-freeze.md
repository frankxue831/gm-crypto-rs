# v1.15 calibration readiness and date declaration

The fixed calibration cohort is **September 29 through November 9, 2026 UTC**:
C0 is `2026-09-29T00:00:00Z`, and the exclusive endpoint C0+42 days is
`2026-11-10T00:00:00Z`. The two 21-day halves end at October 20 and November 10.
This declaration must be reviewed and merged before C0; a missed deadline
requires a new prospective declaration, never retrospective inclusion.

The [execution freeze](v1.15-execution-freeze.json) supplies the dates consumed
by the scheduled capture route. The accepted [protocol](v1.15-protocol.md),
revision `05035d52585b345a5392ff24dd06e3017aa77d36`, determines eligibility,
selection, coverage and numerical outcomes. First scheduled attempts are
selected by actual UTC job-start date for each exact feature leg before
validity is examined. Failed, missing or incompatible draws remain reported;
there is no replacement, early stopping or extension. The existing 05:00 UTC
schedule, four legs, 40-minute limit and five 100000-sample passes are unchanged.
No manual study timing is authorized by this declaration.

## Historical census compatibility

The live readiness audit found that the earliest scheduled job in
[run 25621421256](https://github.com/frankxue831/gm-crypto-rs/actions/runs/25621421256),
May 10, used the older name `timing-leak nightly (100K samples, |tau|<0.20)`.
Complete-census replay previously required the modern feature-leg name even
for a job whose UTC start already proved it outside the calibration window.
This declaration also repairs that reporting failure: validate the UTC start
first, retain outside-window jobs with their original names as excluded rows,
and require an exact feature identity for every in-window scheduled first
attempt. Missing ordering metadata still stops derivation; no job is dropped
or substituted. A full transport-to-candidate regression demonstrates the old
failure and verifies that the retained historical row adds no coverage and
changes no proposed bound.

## Limits and subsequent decision

This is permission to collect the fixed calibration cohort, not a candidate
table, evidence of sensitivity or permission to activate a new gate. T0 stays
null. Candidate derivation waits for the full interval and completed selected
jobs. A nonempty proposed set still requires a reviewed candidate freeze,
prospective evaluator and isolated control execution readiness, then its own
42-day confirmation window and all predeclared controls. An empty proposed
set follows the protocol's insufficient-evidence outcome without inventing T0.

The [September 17 sentinel breach](v1.15-sentinel-investigation.md) and
[original smoke alarm](v1.15-smoke-diagnostic.md) remain unexplained. The single
predeclared D1 diagnostic is complete and is not repeated. These limits remain
in the study report, and unresolved relevant alarms block activation. Existing
gates remain unchanged; no crate, version or publishing change is included.

## Study isolation amendment (September 30, 2026)

The study freezes its measured source checkout, not the development branch.
The independently reviewed `v1.15-isolation.json` binds the original execution
freeze to a full immutable source revision, the revised launcher workflow and
its helper. The source revision contains the already-qualified crypto, harness,
lock/build identities and original producer; those six identities per leg and
`v1.15-execution-freeze.json` remain byte-identical. No threshold changes.

After this amendment is merged and its four-leg build-only preflight passes,
main may accept crate, manifest, compiler and other feature changes through
ordinary review and CI. The former main-wide source freeze no longer applies.
The isolation manifest, launcher and evidence rules remain reviewed study
inputs; changing them is a further amendment, never routine feature work.

The existing `dudect-nightly.yml` workflow ID, scheduled time, job names and
four feature legs continue the study census. Its launcher checks out main for
orchestration and the pinned revision into a sibling directory for measurement.
Main is never an ancestor of the measured checkout, so its Cargo configuration
cannot affect or invalidate the frozen build.
The archive records the measured source SHA, while authenticated run/artifact
records retain the launcher SHA. Binding verifies both identities through the
reviewed workflow/helper bytes; it never pretends the Actions head is the
measured checkout. Manual dispatch is a build-only preflight, and reruns and
out-of-window jobs cannot launch study timing. The original frozen producer
still enforces its compiler, source, lock, resolution, flags, timing budget and
before/after hashes. Missing or altered identities fail closed.

The original September 29–November 9 cohort remains fixed. Legacy captures
qualify only under the original workflow and producer identity; isolated
captures qualify only under the separately reviewed launcher and helper.
This amendment is prospective: it must merge before the first isolated
scheduled measurement. Earlier compatible captures remain in the same census;
failed, missing and incompatible first attempts remain exclusions, with no
replacement or extension. Selection occurs before examining validity or timing.
Reports retain each run's launcher SHA and measured source SHA and the amendment
hash, making the orchestration transition auditable. No new timing outcomes
were used to choose this change; its purpose is to unblock development.

`dudect-main.yml` independently checks the current main revision every night
with the existing production gates. At introduction its parser is byte-identical
to the former production parser. PR smoke is unchanged. Production runs never
supply study coverage or validate a candidate on new code. The additional
production route adds four jobs, each capped at 40 minutes, per scheduled night
while both routes are active; study sampling remains five 100000-sample passes
per feature leg. The study route executes no timing after its declared endpoint.

Replay the complete existing workflow census using the original execution
freeze plus `--isolation docs/v1.15-isolation.json`. Collector schema 2 retains
the launcher helper source from each authenticated run revision; schema 1
archives remain replayable but cannot qualify isolated runs without that source.
The confirmation CLI accepts `--calibration-isolation` to reproduce this
calibration report. Confirmation execution still requires its own reviewed
candidate/producer readiness and prospective dates; this amendment starts no
confirmation window and activates no candidate.

Results remain applicable only to the validated source/build and runner
identities. A newer main revision does not inherit these bounds. Applying a
candidate there requires a separate applicability review and any necessary
fresh validation; existing gates remain authoritative until then. The original
unexplained alarms and injected-control requirements are unchanged.
