# v1.15 calibration readiness and date declaration

The fixed calibration cohort is **September 29 through November 9, 2026 UTC**:
C0 is `2026-09-29T00:00:00Z`, and the exclusive endpoint C0+42 days is
`2026-11-10T00:00:00Z`. The two 21-day halves end at October 20 and November 10.
This declaration must be reviewed and merged before C0; a missed deadline
requires a new prospective declaration, never retrospective inclusion.

The [execution freeze](v1.15-execution-freeze.json) supplies the dates consumed
by the scheduled capture route. The accepted [protocol](v1.15-protocol.md),
revision `05035d52585b345a5392ff24dd06e3017aa77d36`, determines eligibility,
selection, coverage and numerical outcomes. First scheduled attempts are
selected by actual UTC job-start date for each exact feature leg before
validity is examined. Failed, missing or incompatible draws remain reported;
there is no replacement, early stopping or extension. The existing 05:00 UTC
schedule, four legs, 40-minute limit and five 100000-sample passes are unchanged.
No manual study timing is authorized by this declaration.

## Historical census compatibility

The live readiness audit found that the earliest scheduled job in
[run 25621421256](https://github.com/frankxue831/gm-crypto-rs/actions/runs/25621421256),
May 10, used the older name `timing-leak nightly (100K samples, |tau|<0.20)`.
Complete-census replay previously required the modern feature-leg name even
for a job whose UTC start already proved it outside the calibration window.
This declaration also repairs that reporting failure: validate the UTC start
first, retain outside-window jobs with their original names as excluded rows,
and require an exact feature identity for every in-window scheduled first
attempt. Missing ordering metadata still stops derivation; no job is dropped
or substituted. A full transport-to-candidate regression demonstrates the old
failure and verifies that the retained historical row adds no coverage and
changes no proposed bound.

## Limits and subsequent decision

This is permission to collect the fixed calibration cohort, not a candidate
table, evidence of sensitivity or permission to activate a new gate. T0 stays
null. Candidate derivation waits for the full interval and completed selected
jobs. A nonempty proposed set still requires a reviewed candidate freeze,
prospective evaluator and isolated control execution readiness, then its own
42-day confirmation window and all predeclared controls. An empty proposed
set follows the protocol's insufficient-evidence outcome without inventing T0.

The [September 17 sentinel breach](v1.15-sentinel-investigation.md) and
[original smoke alarm](v1.15-smoke-diagnostic.md) remain unexplained. The single
predeclared D1 diagnostic is complete and is not repeated. These limits remain
in the study report, and unresolved relevant alarms block activation. Existing
gates remain unchanged; no crate, version or publishing change is included.
